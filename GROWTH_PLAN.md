# Growth & Promotion Strategy for NarrativeAI

## 📋 Complete Action Plan

### Phase 1: Landing Page & SEO (Week 1)
- ✅ High-conversion landing page with hero, features, social proof
- ✅ Blog section (5-10 SEO-optimized articles)
- ✅ Google Analytics & conversion tracking
- ✅ Schema markup for search engines
- ✅ Sitemap & robots.txt

### Phase 2: Content Marketing (Week 2-4)
Blog post topics (SEO-optimized):
1. "How to Convert PDFs to Audiobooks: Complete Guide"
2. "Best Text-to-Speech Tools for Students in 2026"
3. "Why Professional Voices Matter in Audiobook Production"
4. "Turn Your Thesis into an Audiobook in 30 Minutes"
5. "Free PDF to Audio Converter: NarrativeAI vs Competitors"

### Phase 3: Social Media & Viral Growth (Week 2-8)
**Reddit Strategy:**
- r/productivity: "I built a free PDF-to-audio tool, no sign-up needed"
- r/students: "Convert lecture notes to audio instantly"
- r/audiobooks: "Create audiobooks from any PDF for free"

**Twitter/X Strategy:**
- Daily: 30-second demo clips
- Weekly: "X reasons why audiobooks improve learning"
- Threads: Technical deep-dives on AI voices

**TikTok/YouTube Shorts:**
- 15-30 second demos: Upload → Download in seconds
- Before/After audio quality
- Student use cases

### Phase 4: Paid Acquisition (Week 3+)
**Google Ads:**
- Budget: $500-1000/month
- Keywords: "PDF to audio", "text to speech", "audiobook maker"
- Target CPC: $0.50-2.00

**Facebook Ads:**
- Audiences: Students, professionals, content creators, entrepreneurs
- Creative: "Listen to PDFs while driving"
- Lookalike audiences from website visitors

### Phase 5: Analytics & Optimization (Ongoing)
**Track:**
- Page views, bounce rate, time on site
- Click-through rates (CTR)
- Conversion funnel: Landing → App → Upload → Download
- User demographics, traffic sources
- A/B testing results

**Key Metrics to Target:**
- Landing page CTR: 5%+ (benchmark: 2-3%)
- App conversion rate: 30%+ (benchmark: 10-20%)
- Cost per acquisition: <$5 (revenue allows)

### Phase 6: Partnerships & Integrations (Week 8+)
**Zapier Integration:** "PDF from Email → Convert to Audio"
**Google Drive Integration:** "Right-click any PDF → Convert"
**API Access:** Let developers build on top of NarrativeAI
